## Vision

**Make tools people can use, so AI shows up in study and work.**

A good tool should be simple, reliable, and easy to use. The products are open source. Talk with us, change the code with us, and tell us when something is wrong.

## Projects

:link[Agentero]{id=https://agentero.app img=/logos/agentero.svg .square} is where people and agents keep a literature library together. The research context stays local.

::github[poco-ai/Agentero]

- :link[Poco]{#poco-ai/poco-claw} won bronze at the national final of :link[AI Hackathon Tour]{id=https://modelscope.cn/active/ai-hackathon-tour} in 2026. It is a coworker you can keep in a pocket. You talk to it on the web and in messengers. The work runs in a sandbox.
- :link[YumGo]{#poco-ai/YumGo} placed second at the Yangtze River Delta campus stop of :link[Douyin AI Creator]{id=https://aiia.douyin.com/}. It keeps a taste you have had and turns it into the next route out.
- :link[Clip]{#poco-ai/Clip} puts invoices, attachments, and claim progress in one flow. It recognizes receipts, drops duplicates, pairs the files, and exports the claim.
- :link[TokenArena]{#poco-ai/TokenArena} collects token use in one place. It reads local AI command-line usage and shows it by day, tool, and model at :link[token.poco-ai.com]{id=https://token.poco-ai.com}.
- :link[Parqview]{#poco-ai/Parqview} opens Parquet on this machine. The file stays local, in a table, one record at a time, or as JSON.
